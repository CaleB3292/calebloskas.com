About

From top performer to team multiplier.

I’m a Senior Account Executive and Team Lead at a connected-operations technology company, leading complex, multi-stakeholder sales with mid-market organizations across operations, safety, and fleet.

I was promoted to Team Lead within a year. Today I still carry a full quota while leading in-person training for new hires, coaching new and tenured reps on pipeline and deal strategy, and partnering with product leaders to run the first sales cycles for beta and pre-release products.

Before that, I spent seven years building a foundation in consulting, business development, insurance, and customer-experience software, which shaped how I approach problems and why I focus on long-term customer value.

What drives me is making other people better: simplifying difficult problems, earning trust, and building teams where top performers raise the standard for everyone, not just their own number.

Outside of work, I’m a husband and a dad of three, and most of my time goes to my family. I’m always learning and building something, whether that’s ham radio, woodworking, or 3D printing.
